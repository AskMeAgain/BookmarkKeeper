Bookmark Keeper

Annoyed that you lose all your bookmarks when switching tasks or branches in intellij? This plugin hooks into the task loading & branch switching functionality and keeps all bookmarks where they belong. Since version 2.0.0 this plugin also keeps track of files which **dont exist on branches** and will restore them when they are available again, essentially fixing https://youtrack.jetbrains.com/issue/IJPL-62074 (13 year old bug). Note: Sometimes bookmarks are not immediately available after restoring. Give it a few seconds and they work (no idea why)
